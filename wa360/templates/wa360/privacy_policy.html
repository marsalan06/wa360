1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard information when organizations connect their WhatsApp Business accounts to our SaaS platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Organization profile data provided by administrators.
  • WhatsApp Business identifiers returned by Meta (WABA ID, phone number ID, display number).
  • Message metadata required for automation (timestamps, delivery status).

3. How We Use Information

  • To enable outbound and inbound WhatsApp messaging on behalf of your organization.
  • To provide analytics, conversation history, and AI-assisted replies.
  • To maintain security, audit logs, and compliance with Meta platform policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with Meta Platforms, Inc. and our subprocessors strictly as required to deliver the WhatsApp Cloud API functionality.

5. Data Security

Access tokens and sensitive credentials are stored encrypted. Access is limited to authorized personnel and protected by role-based controls.

6. Your Rights

Organizations may request deletion or export of data at any time by contacting our support team. End users can opt out of automated messaging by replying STOP or by contacting the organization administrator.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, email support@example.com.
